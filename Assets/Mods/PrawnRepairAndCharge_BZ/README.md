# Prawn Suit Repair and Charge BZ

Ever wondered why the Moonpool and Seatruck docking facilities don't repair and recharge your Prawn Suit? It turns out, Alterra offer that functionality as an "added extra". You know, like how car manufacturers charge you for "unlocking" basic functionality like GPS, onboard media support, a steering wheel etc.

We think you deserve the full value of your investment in your new Moonpool or Seatruck, so we're offering you this opportunity to unlock the full repair and recharge potential of these facilities at no extra cost to you!

## Features

- Fully charges and repairs your Prawnsuit on successful docking either to the SeaTruck Dock or in a Moonpool.
- Can be configured to optionally draw power from the SeaTruck or Base, with configurable modifiers for both charge and repair.

## User Guide

Dock a Prawn Suit in a Moonpool or Seatruck Dock to repair and recharge it - simple!

## Options

Configure the mod under Options > Mods:

- **Enable In MoonPool**  - enable to repair and charge when docking at a Moonpool.
- **Enable In SeaTruck** - enable to repair and charge when docking at a Seatruck.
- **Consume Base Power** - enable to draw proportionate power from the base when docking with at a MoonPool.
- **Consume SeaTruck Power** - enable to draw proportionate power from the base when docking with at a Seatruck.
- **Charge Power Cost Modifier** - sets the energy cost per unit of charge added for either docking location.
- **Repair Power Cost Modifier** - sets the energy cost per unit of health restored for either docking location.
- **Detailed logging** - only enable this if you have an issue and want to provide useful logging when reporting a bug.
