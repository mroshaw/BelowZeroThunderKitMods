# Automatic Locker Label Categories

This document lists the categories used when automatically generating locker labels and every `TechType` assigned to them. An item may belong to more than one category.

## Categories

- Metals
- Crystals
- Batteries
- Electronics
- Food
- Water
- Tools
- Equipment
- Materials
- Vehicle Upgrades
- Eggs
- Creatures
- Collectibles
- Raw Materials
