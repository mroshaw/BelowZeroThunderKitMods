[size=4][b]User Guide[/b][/size]
[size=3]With this new Subnautica Pets mod, I've rebuilt from the ground up, introducing "lore-friendly" gameplay around the "Pets" concept. With this in mind, this mod is ideally played "as is", with discovery playing a part in getting the most out of the mod. As such, I recommend you play the mod in "Adventure" mode! When it comes to collecting resources, don't forget the Scanner Room and Scanner Room Chip - scanning for resources is your friend!

That said, if you're in the endgame and just want to enjoy having some beasties running around your base, you can enable "Creative" mode in the mod options to unlock everything. All you need then is titanium, and you can explore all that the mod has to offer. Switching modes requires a restart of the game but can be done mid-save.

Note that "Custom Pets" don't require DNA, and are not a part of the "Adventure" lore-friendly experience.

Unlike previous versions, there are no other "mod options" or keyboard shortcuts. All mod functionality is built into the game.[/size]
